How prediction and priority scoring work

Timeline Horizon: Estimated resolution duration is calculated using a Gradient Boosting Regressor trained on Indian jurisdictional timelines. Features include statutory case type, days elapsed since filing date, number of active parties, appeal flag, and semantic precedent similarity statistics.

Precedent Features: Query embeddings are evaluated against the corpus index to derive top-1 cosine similarity, mean of top-5 matches, matches surpassing threshold, and similarity spread.

Priority Score: Weighted combination of time pending, hearing count overhead, and precedent ambiguity. Higher score indicates matters needing expedited judicial intervention.
